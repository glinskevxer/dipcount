# What the district means

Generated from `dipcount.glossary` by
`python -m dipcount.tools.build_glossary`. Edit the term table, not
this file.

22 terms.

| term | defined in | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| answer time | `dipcount.model.request` | How long the district has to get out to a call. It comes off the kind of call, and a busy virus week shortens two of them. |
| block | `dipcount.model.block` | The ground a fogging truck covers in one pass of a street grid. Measured in acres for the rate and in road miles for the driving. |
| calibration | `dipcount.model.equipment` | Flow, droplet size and swath measured together. With the driving speed they say what actually went on the ground. |
| diagnostic time | `dipcount.model.bioassay` | The minute a susceptible population is flat by. What is still standing then is the resistance finding. |
| dip | `dipcount.model.inspection` | One stroke of the dipper. The count of larvae over the count of dips is the figure the larval threshold is written in. |
| habitat | `dipcount.habitat` | What kind of water a site is. It decides how the site is measured, how often it is looked at, and what may legally go into it. |
| index | `dipcount.service.trapping` | Females per trap night. Males are counted and left out of it, because a male mosquito neither bites nor carries anything. |
| infection rate | `dipcount.estimate` | An estimate, not a count, because pools are tested rather than mosquitoes. Quoted per thousand. |
| instar | `dipcount.model.inspection` | One of the four larval stages. First and second mean the treatment window is open; third and fourth mean it is closing. |
| label | `dipcount.model.product` | What the product may be applied to, at what rate range, how often, and how much one place may take in a season. Federal law. |
| lot | `dipcount.model.lot` | One batch of one product as it arrived, under one manufacturer code. Everything applied comes out of a lot, so a recall can be answered. |
| mode of action | `dipcount.rules` | The group an active ingredient belongs to. Rotation is over these and not over brand names. |
| notice | `dipcount.model.block` | One place told about one block for one window, far enough ahead for the kind of entry it is. |
| pool | `dipcount.model.pool` | Up to fifty females of one species out of one jar, tested together. A positive pool says at least one was carrying, not how many. |
| pupa | `dipcount.model.inspection` | The stage after the fourth instar. It does not feed, so no larvicide reaches it, and a brood that is mostly pupae is past treating. |
| register | `dipcount.model.exclusion` | The places the district does not spray over. Some want notice and some take the block off the run whatever anybody was told. |
| reporting week | `dipcount.clock` | Sunday to Saturday, numbered so that week one holds four or more days of January. Everything the state adds up is keyed on it. |
| response level | `dipcount.rules` | Where the vector index puts the district, from none to emergency. It shortens two of the service request clocks. |
| season cap | `dipcount.service.larvicide` | The most one acre or one structure may receive in a year. What is left of it limits the rate, and can leave no legal rate at all. |
| site | `dipcount.model.site` | A piece of water on the inspection list. Measured in acres or in structures depending on its habitat, and never in both. |
| trap night | `dipcount.model.trap` | One trap out for one night. The denominator of every adult index, and it excludes any trap that did not run. |
| vector index | `dipcount.estimate` | Abundance times infection rate: how many infected females a trap would take in a night. The response ladder reads this. |
